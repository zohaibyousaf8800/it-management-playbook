## Conditions of use

By signing below, the user confirms that they:

1. Have received the asset and accessories listed above, in working condition.
2. Will use the asset for [Company] business in line with the acceptable use policy.
3. Will not install unapproved software, and will keep the device's security controls (encryption, endpoint protection, screen lock) enabled.
4. Will report loss, theft or damage to the service desk immediately.
5. Will return the asset, with accessories, on request or on their last working day.
